HANZI.OS filters the source to official HSK2 surfaces, normalizes numbered
Pinyin for exact matching, and stores compact English source senses plus
source-line hashes. Vietnamese glosses, examples and review decisions are not
copied from CC-CEDICT and remain separate pending editorial work.
