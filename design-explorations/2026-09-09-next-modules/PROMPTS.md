## 01-ngoc-lenh

Use case: ui-mockup. Produce an ultra-polished high-fidelity original HANZI.OS Vietnamese Mandarin-learning web interface design board. Front-on flat UI, not perspective, no devices, no photography of screens. Large landscape image with TWO full desktop screens stacked vertically, each wide 16:9, small theme label outside screens. Both screens must be complete and legible, no cropped CTAs. Common compact left navigation with five labels Học, Ôn, Nói, Luyện, Hồ sơ. Top screen title "Thiên Cơ Kính", subtitle "Nhìn rõ hành trình của bạn". Show weekly activity bar chart with dates and axis labels, lesson progress labeled "Bài đã hoàn thành" (not mastery), seven skill rows Âm / Pinyin, Nghe, Nói, Đọc, Hán tự, Từ vựng, Ngữ pháp with distinct evidence states including "Chưa đủ bằng chứng". One primary CTA "Tiếp tục học". Lower screen is "Chi tiết kỹ năng" with selected "Từ vựng", evidence over time chart, practiced words table and action "Ôn từ đến hạn". Activity/XP clearly distinct from skill evidence, no arbitrary overall mastery percentage or native-speaker accuracy claims. Design data illustrative only, no fake testimonials. Vietnamese headings spelled carefully, simple text and rational spacing. Beautiful bespoke art direction but panels, type, charts and borders must be reproducible in CSS/SVG; decorative art confined to backdrop outside data. No visual clutter, no glowing text, no overlapping labels, no scrollbars on these overview screens.
Theme label "Ngọc Lệnh". Specific art direction: deep jade lacquer, champagne gold, milky jade circular inlays, elegant scholarly luxury. Make this a genuinely distinct design, not just a recolor: appropriate composition, chart treatment, panel shape and typographic hierarchy.
